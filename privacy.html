If you turn on reminders, we store an approximate area (rounded to about 11 km) so the reminder can mention rain or cold. You can delete everything we hold at any time from Settings.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Ready is operated by [INSERT YOUR FULL LEGAL NAME] ("we", "us"), acting as the data controller. For any privacy question or request, contact [INSERT CONTACT EMAIL ADDRESS].

Ready is currently a small, non-commercial pilot. We do not sell data, we do not run advertising, and we do not share data for cross-context behavioural advertising.

2. What stays only on your device

The following is stored in your browser's local storage and is never transmitted to us:

  • Your exact location (the most recent usable coordinates, cached so the checklist can load without asking again).
  • Your clothing preferences — the specific garments you told Ready you own.
  • Your settings: expected time away, reminder time, language, and onboarding progress.

Clearing your browser data or using "Delete my data" in Settings removes all of it.

3. What we collect and why

4. Your precise location and the weather service

This is the one case where precise data leaves your device, so we want to be explicit about it. To fetch a forecast, your browser sends your coordinates directly to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), a third-party weather API, at roughly 11-metre precision. This request goes from your device to Open-Meteo — it does not pass through our servers, and we never receive or store those precise coordinates. Open-Meteo's own privacy terms govern that request.

Separately, and only if you turn on reminders, we store a deliberately coarsened version of your location (rounded to about 11 km) on our servers. Rounding happens on your device before anything is sent.

5. Who else processes your data

  • Cloudflare — hosts the app and its API, and runs the scheduled reminder jobs. Like any web host, it processes your IP address and request metadata.
  • Supabase — provides the database where the data in section 3 is stored.
  • Open-Meteo — receives forecast requests as described in section 4.
  • Apple and/or Google push services — whichever issued your browser's push subscription relays reminder notifications to your device.

These providers act as our processors or as independent controllers of their own infrastructure data. We do not control their systems, and while we choose established providers, we cannot guarantee their security. See section 9.

6. Automated processing and machine learning

Ready's clothing recommendations are produced by fixed, human-written rules — thresholds for temperature, rain probability, and wind. There is no machine learning model, no profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and your data is never used to train any AI model.

We do run a forecast-accuracy check: we record what the weather service predicted for a coarse area and what actually occurred, to measure how reliable forecasts are. Those records are keyed to an area, not to you, and are never used to change recommendations automatically — any change requires a human to review and approve it.

7. Your rights and how to use them

Depending on where you live — including under Ecuador's Ley Orgánica de Protección de Datos Personales and the California Consumer Privacy Act — you have rights to access, correct, delete, and port your data, and to object to processing.

The fastest way to exercise deletion is in the app: open Settings and choose Delete my data. This immediately removes every record tied to your installation ID from our database, cancels any scheduled reminders, and clears local storage on your device.

For access, correction, portability, or any other request, email [INSERT CONTACT EMAIL ADDRESS]. We will respond within 30 days. Because Ready has no accounts, we may need you to provide your installation ID (shown in Settings) to locate your records — we cannot identify you any other way, and we will not ask you for additional identifying documents.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of in that respect. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any right.

8. Retention

We keep pilot data for as long as the pilot is running, and for no longer than [INSERT RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. "12 months"] after collection, after which it is deleted or aggregated so it can no longer be linked to a device. You can delete your data sooner at any time from Settings.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS and stored by Supabase with encryption at rest. Database access requires a server-side key that is never present in the app you run; row-level security is enabled on every table, with no public access policies. Access to production data is limited to the operator named in section 1.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot and do not guarantee absolute security. We do not warrant that our providers' systems will never be breached. If a breach affects your data, we will notify affected users and any required authority as the law requires.

10. Children

Ready is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used Ready, contact us and we will delete the data.

11. International transfers

Our providers operate globally, so your data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. We rely on those providers' standard contractual protections for such transfers.

12. Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will update the date above and surface a notice in the app. Continuing to use Ready after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Esa solicitud va de tu dispositivo a Open-Meteo: no pasa por nuestros servidores y nunca recibimos ni guardamos esas coordenadas precisas. Los términos de privacidad de Open-Meteo rigen esa solicitud.

Por separado, y solo si activas los recordatorios, guardamos en nuestros servidores una versión deliberadamente redondeada de tu ubicación (a unos 11 km). El redondeo ocurre en tu dispositivo antes de enviar nada.

6. Procesamiento automatizado y aprendizaje automático

Las recomendaciones de ropa de Ready se generan con reglas fijas escritas por personas: umbrales de temperatura, probabilidad de lluvia y viento. No hay ningún modelo de aprendizaje automático, no hay elaboración de perfiles con efectos jurídicos o similarmente significativos, y tus datos nunca se usan para entrenar ningún modelo de IA.

Sí realizamos una verificación de precisión del pronóstico: registramos lo que el servicio del clima predijo para una zona aproximada y lo que realmente ocurrió, para medir qué tan confiables son los pronósticos. Esos registros se asocian a una zona, no a ti, y nunca se usan para cambiar recomendaciones automáticamente: cualquier cambio requiere revisión y aprobación humana.
